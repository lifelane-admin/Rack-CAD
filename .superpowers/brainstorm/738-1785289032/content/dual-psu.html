Dual PSU — Last N Holes = Power Inlets

Option A scales cleanly. The device declares psuCount and the last N ports become power inlets.

Single PSU (psuCount: 1)
Dual PSU (psuCount: 2) — HA config

Rule: last N ports = power inlets

DeviceTemplate gets a psuCount: 1 | 2 field.
At render time: ports [totalPorts - psuCount] onwards are power inlets — rendered with a C14/IEC inlet graphic instead of an RJ45 jack.
A power cable drawn from a PSU inlet to a PDU outlet is typed as cableType: 'power' and carries the plug sub-category (C13, IS 6538, etc.).

Single PSU → only A-feed used, right PDU slot stays empty (or unconnected).
Dual PSU → PSU1 → Left PDU (red), PSU2 → Right PDU (blue) = HA.
