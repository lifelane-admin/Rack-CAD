Switch Panel: Current vs. New Design

The rackstudio-demo.jsx introduces stacked-pair port layout, block grouping, rich gradients, a detailed console section, and rack ears. Pick which visual elements you want carried over into panel-renderer.ts.

Current — panel-renderer.ts (24-port switch)
  • Simple LED strip + flat rect socket, 3 pins
  • Single row; all ports at same height
  • Status section left (power LED, alarm, console)
  • SFP cages: plain dark rect + cyan glow at bottom
  • No rack ears, no brand area
New — rackstudio-demo.jsx style (24-port switch, 4U-scale render)

Note: rendered at 4× scale above for visibility. The real 1U panel is 30px tall in the rack.

  • Rack mounting ears with screw holes
  • Console section: 4 status LEDs + brand wordmark + console RJ45
  • Stacked-pair ports (odd top/even bottom), 8 gold contacts, oriented tabs
  • Block grouping with visible gap every 4 columns
  • SFP section with separator line + "SFP UPLINK" label + module renders
  • Rich multi-stop gradients on chassis, cages, contacts
