Layout Question: How do blades map to our fixed-width device slot?

In the JSX, blades fill to a fixed 19" total width. Our panel-renderer.ts always receives W=350px (the inner rack slot). We need to decide how to map the blade concept into that fixed space.

A

No blades — full-width chassis interior

Use the full 350px for chassis content. Left edge starts the console section directly. Matches how all other device panels (servers, firewalls, PDUs) currently work. Simpler implementation, consistent with the rest of the codebase.

Console zone (55px) | Stacked RJ45 pairs | SFP | Vents — all within 350px

B

Blade margins within the device slot

Reserve a fixed left+right blade margin (e.g. 24px each) inside the 350px slot, styled like the JSX folded flange. Chassis interior uses the remaining 302px. Visually matches the JSX reference, but compresses the port area and differs from how other device types render.

Left blade (24px) | Chassis interior (302px) | Right blade (24px) — total 350px

C

Compute BLADE_W dynamically like the JSX

Mirror the JSX formula: compute chassis content width from port count, set BLADE_W = (W − chassisW) / 2. Small switches (8-port) get wide blades with only a few ports; 48-port gets minimal blade margins. This is the most faithful to the reference design but makes the renderer significantly more complex.
